Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Roshni (روشنی). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of our visually impaired users. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • Authentication Data: We use Firebase Authentication to manage secure logins (email/phone). We store your basic profile information (Name, Phone Number) securely in Cloud Firestore.
  • Camera & Audio Processing: Roshni requires Camera and Microphone permissions to function. We do not secretly record, store, or sell your photos or audio. Most AI processing (Object Detection, Currency Classification, Document Scanning) happens locally on your device (Edge AI).
  • Cloud Vision Features: For complex Scene Description and Urdu OCR, encrypted images may be sent to our secure Cloud Vision providers (Groq API). These images are processed instantly and immediately discarded; they are never used for training third-party models.

3. Local Storage & Cache

We use local storage (SharedPreferences) and offline caching to store your scan history for up to 1 week. This data lives on your device to ensure you can access your history without an internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app relies on Google Firebase (Auth/Firestore) and Google ML Kit. Please refer to Google's official privacy policies regarding their infrastructure.
